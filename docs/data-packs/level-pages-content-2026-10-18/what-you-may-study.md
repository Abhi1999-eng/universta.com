# What you may study (level pages, 2026-10-18)

Written in our own words. Shown on every level page of the specialization ("What you may study"). Lead line, then the topics as a bulleted list.

## Data Science

Data science programmes combine statistics, programming and domain knowledge, teaching you to gather, clean and analyse data and explain what it shows.

- Probability and statistics
- Mathematics for data analysis
- Programming in Python and R
- Databases and SQL
- Data cleaning and preparation
- Machine learning methods
- Data visualisation and communication
- Big data and cloud computing
- Data ethics and privacy
- Applied research project

## Artificial Intelligence

Artificial intelligence programmes look at how computers can learn, reason and perceive, and teach you to build, test and evaluate such systems.

- Mathematics for AI
- Programming and algorithms
- Machine learning
- Deep learning and neural networks
- Natural language processing
- Computer vision
- Search, planning and reasoning
- Robotics and intelligent agents
- AI ethics and safety
- Applied research project
