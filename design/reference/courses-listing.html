Resources & guides

How to choose the right study abroad course

The best program balances four things: academic fit, affordability, admission chances and career outcomes. Start by filtering on your target degree level and destination, then narrow by tuition band and scholarship availability. Shortlist three to five courses and use the compare tray to weigh rankings, employability and post-study work rights against total cost.

Popular course & destination combinations

Long-tail searches like MBA in Canada, Data Science in Australia, Nursing in the UK, Artificial Intelligence in Germany and Master's in Finance in Ireland each open a dedicated, filterable listing.

English language requirements

Most universities accept IELTS, TOEFL, PTE or Duolingo. Requirements vary by program and level — filter by your test and score to see only courses you're eligible for, and check whether IELTS-optional pathways apply to your destination.
