Why study in Canada in 2026

Six reasons Canada stays on most Indian students' shortlists — with the numbers behind each one.

A real route to PR

Express Entry awards points for Canadian study and Canadian work experience, so your degree directly improves your residency score.

Most graduates apply within 3 years

Three-year work permit

A two-year qualifying programme earns a Post-Graduation Work Permit of up to three years — time enough to build the experience PR asks for.

PGWP up to 36 months

Lower total cost

Co-op built into the degree

Family can come with you

Study permit

Student visa requirements for Canada in 2026

Canada issues a study permit rather than a student visa. Under the Student Direct Stream, decisions typically come faster for Indian applicants.

1

Secure your letter of acceptance

It must come from a Designated Learning Institution. Programmes at non-designated institutions do not qualify for a study permit.

2

Get your Provincial Attestation Letter

The province issues this against its allocated cap. Your institution requests it once you accept the offer — request it early, caps fill.

The most common cause of delay
3

Buy a Guaranteed Investment Certificate

A GIC of the required amount from a participating bank proves you can cover your first year of living costs. The bank releases it to you in instalments after arrival.

4

Pay tuition and gather financial proof

Under SDS you usually pay the first year's tuition upfront. Keep receipts and bank statements consistent with what you declare.

5

Complete the medical and biometrics

The medical must be done by an IRCC-approved panel physician. Biometrics are given at a Visa Application Centre after you apply.

6

Submit online and track

Apply through the IRCC portal with all documents attached. Incomplete files are returned rather than refused, but you lose weeks.

SDS decisions often within a few weeks

Immigration rules change frequently. Always confirm current requirements on the official IRCC website before applying.

Common questions

Frequently asked questions

Is IELTS compulsory to study in Canada?
Not always. Many universities accept TOEFL, PTE or Duolingo, and some waive the test entirely if your degree was taught in English. The Student Direct Stream, however, requires an IELTS band of 6.0 in each module — so if you want faster visa processing, IELTS is effectively necessary.
How much money do I need to show for a Canadian study permit?
Beyond tuition, you must show living funds of roughly CA$20,635 for a single applicant outside Quebec, usually held as a Guaranteed Investment Certificate. This figure is revised periodically — confirm the current amount on the IRCC site before you buy the GIC.
Can I work while studying in Canada?
Yes. Study permit holders enrolled full-time at a designated institution may work off campus during term, with unlimited hours during scheduled breaks. The weekly cap during term has changed more than once recently, so check the current limit rather than relying on older guidance.
Is a postgraduate diploma as good as a master's degree?
For employment and PGWP purposes, a PG diploma from a public college is well regarded and considerably cheaper. For academic progression — a PhD, or roles requiring a research background — a master's carries more weight. Choose based on whether your goal is work or further study.
How long does a Canadian study permit take?
Under the Student Direct Stream, decisions often arrive within a few weeks for complete applications. Standard processing takes considerably longer and varies by season. The Provincial Attestation Letter, not IRCC processing, is the most common source of delay.
Can my spouse work in Canada while I study?
Spouses of students in many full-time graduate and professional programmes qualify for an open work permit. Eligibility narrowed in recent policy changes and now depends on your programme level, so verify against your specific course before planning around it.
Does studying in Canada guarantee permanent residency?
No. It creates a strong pathway — Canadian study and work experience both add points under Express Entry — but PR depends on your overall score, the occupation you work in, and the cut-offs at the time you apply. Treat it as a good route, not a guaranteed one.
