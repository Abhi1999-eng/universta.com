About Computer Science

What is Computer Science?

Computer Science is the study of computation — how problems are solved with algorithms, how data is structured and processed, and how software and intelligent systems are designed and built. It spans theory (logic, complexity, algorithms) and practice (software engineering, AI, security, systems).

Why study this subject?

It's among the most in-demand and highest-paid fields globally, with roles across every industry and strong post-study work and PR pathways in top destinations. The skills transfer widely and rarely go out of date.

History, global importance & future scope

From its mathematical roots in the mid-20th century to today's AI revolution, Computer Science now underpins healthcare, finance, transport, entertainment and government. Emerging areas — generative AI, quantum computing, edge and autonomous systems — point to decades of continued demand.
